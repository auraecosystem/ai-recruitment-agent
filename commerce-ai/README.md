# COMMERCE AI

## Tagline

Hire Commerce & Finance Talent 10x Faster with AI

## Executive Summary

COMMERCE AI is an AI-powered hiring copilot designed specifically for commerce, accounting, finance, banking, audit, treasury, tax, procurement, and ERP-driven organizations.

Instead of acting as a generic ATS, COMMERCE AI focuses on one problem:

Finding the best finance candidates faster than traditional hiring processes.

The platform automatically:

* Reads CVs
* Extracts skills and certifications
* Matches candidates against job requirements
* Scores candidates
* Generates interview questions
* Produces hiring recommendations

Target customers:

* Recruitment agencies
* Accounting firms
* SMEs
* Banks
* Insurance companies
* Financial institutions
* Enterprise HR departments

Core value proposition:

Reduce screening time by 70–90% while improving hiring quality.
